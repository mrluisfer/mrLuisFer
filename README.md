### About

I craft scalable frontend architectures and component systems that teams actually want to use. Focused on **accessibility** (WCAG), **performance**, and turning complex requirements into intuitive experiences. Currently at a financial services company in Mexico, previously shipping features at **Nowports** and **Accenture**.

### What I've Built

**@ Nowports** `2022 – 2024`

- 🎨 **Design System** — 50+ accessible components with React, Remix-UI & React Aria
- 🤖 **AI Chatbot Interface** — Conversational UI for shipment tracking & user support
- 📊 **Analytics Infrastructure** — GA4, GTM, cookie management across regions
- 📝 **CMS Integration** — Next.js + HubSpot with SSR for content delivery
- 🔍 **Observability** — Sentry monitoring for error tracking & performance

**@ Accenture** `2021 – 2022`

- Delivered UI/UX improvements across landing pages and product surfaces
- Implemented accessibility standards and component optimizations
